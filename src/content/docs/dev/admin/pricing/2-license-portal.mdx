---
title: License Portal
---

Buy, change, and cancel your ricochet license at [licensing.ricochet.rs](https://licensing.ricochet.rs).

## Create an Account

1. Open [licensing.ricochet.rs](https://licensing.ricochet.rs) and select **Sign in with SSO**.
2. Select **Create an account**, or sign in with GitHub, Microsoft, or Google.
3. Confirm your email address with the code you receive.
4. Set up an authenticator app as your second factor.

## Buy a License

1. Select **Purchase License**.
2. Select a term, the number of apps and tasks, and any capabilities you need, such as the air-gapped add-on for a server without internet access.
3. Enter a license name, such as the domain of your ricochet server.
4. Select **Credit card** to pay now, or **Manual invoice** to receive an invoice for bank transfer.
5. Copy the license key from the license page and [activate it](/dev/admin/pricing/3-activate-license/).

Prices are in US dollars, and you can pay in your local currency at checkout.

## Change Apps, Tasks, or Capabilities

Select **Change features** below a license, set the new counts or capabilities, and select **Calculate price**.

- What you add is charged now as a prorated amount for the rest of your term, and your renewal date stays the same.
- What you remove stays available until your next renewal, which then bills the smaller plan.

While a removal is scheduled, **Change features** shows it, and **Keep current plan** withdraws it.

## Manage Billing

Select **Manage subscription** below a license to open the billing portal.
There you update your payment method, download invoices, and cancel.

## Cancel

Select **Cancel subscription** in the billing portal.
Your license stays active until the end of the paid term, and the license portal shows the date it ends.
After that date, your server runs as the [Community Edition](/dev/admin/pricing/5-community-edition/).

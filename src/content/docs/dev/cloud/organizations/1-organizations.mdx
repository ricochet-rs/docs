An organization owns instances, members, and billing.
Any signed-in user can create one, and the creator becomes its owner.

## Create an organization

<Steps>

1. Select **Organizations** in the sidebar, then **Create organization**.

2. Enter a display name.

3. Accept the suggested slug or type your own.

    A slug is lowercase letters, numbers, and single hyphens, for example `acme-corp`.
    It must be unique across ricochet Cloud.

</Steps>

A new organization starts on a [trial](/dev/cloud/billing/1-trial-and-subscription/) with a baseline resource selection, so you can provision an instance before setting billing up.

## Why the slug matters

The slug names your organization's namespace in the cluster and is baked into every instance hostname.
An instance called `prod` in the organization `acme-corp` answers at `prod-acme-corp.cloud.ricochet.rs`.

You can rename an organization at any time.
You cannot change its slug while it still has instances, because the running instances would be left behind in the old namespace.
Delete the instances first, or change only the display name.

## Switch organizations

The organization switcher sits at the top of the sidebar.
Everything except the Organizations list itself is scoped to the organization you have active.

## Organization logo

Upload a logo under **Settings**.
Until you do, the organization shows its initials.

## Delete an organization

Deleting is owner-only and permanent.
It removes the organization, its instances, and its identity configuration.

Two things block a deletion:

- A live subscription. Cancel it first, so billing stops with the organization.
- An instance that could not be torn down. The deletion stops rather than orphaning it.

You must type the organization's slug to confirm.

<Aside type="caution">
Deleting an organization deletes its instances and the data on them.
There is no undo.
</Aside>

---
title: Email
---

ricochet emails you when it cannot deploy or run your app or task.
It can also email everyone who can open a task after each good run.
Open the item's **Settings** > **Email** tab to set which email ricochet sends.

## Tasks

Select one option under **Email**:

- **Failures and successes**
- **Failures only**, which ricochet sets for every new task
- **Successes only**
- **No email**

ricochet sends failure email to the task's owners, admins and editors.
ricochet puts the end of the log in each one, with a link to the whole log.

ricochet sends success email to everyone who can open the task.
You write it in three fields:

- **Subject**: ricochet replaces `{name}` and `{date}` with the task's name and the run's date. When you leave it empty, ricochet uses `{name} for {date}`.
- **Message**: you write plain text or Markdown.
- **Attachments**: you list one path or glob pattern per line, inside the run's output:

```txt
report.pdf
data/*.csv
```

ricochet attaches up to 20 MiB in total.
Past that, ricochet attaches none of the files and says so in the email.
ricochet never attaches a file or folder whose name starts with a dot, such as `.env`.

## Apps

Turn **Failures** on or off.
ricochet turns it on for every new app, and emails the app's owners, admins and editors when it cannot deploy the app.

## Who gets email

Editors set which email each person and group gets in the **Email** column of the **Collaborators** tab.
Editors select from the same four options as a task.
You can change your own row there too.

- When you have access of your own and through a group, ricochet follows your own row.
- ricochet sends you failure email through a group only when that group can edit the item.
- To cut down email from every item, open your profile and select an option under **Email**. ricochet then sends you only the email that both your profile and the item allow.

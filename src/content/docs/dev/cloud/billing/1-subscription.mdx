---
title: Subscription
---

A new organization starts on a 14-day trial with 2 apps and a 4 GB Compute Pool, so you can create an instance before setting up payment.
Nothing is invoiced during the trial.

To keep your apps running after the trial, open **Billing** and complete checkout.
Checkout ends the trial and starts [metered billing](/dev/cloud/billing/2-metered-billing/).

If the trial ends without a subscription, your instances are suspended.
Nothing is deleted, and completing checkout restores them.

## Failed payments

When a payment fails, your instances keep running, but you cannot create new ones.
After 7 days they become read-only, and 14 days later they are suspended.
An organization that stays suspended for another 14 days has its instances deleted.

Update your payment method through the customer portal link on the **Billing** page to restore everything.

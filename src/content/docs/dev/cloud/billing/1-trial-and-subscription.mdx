A new organization starts on a trial, with a small resource selection already in place, so you can provision an instance before setting up payment.

## The trial

The trial runs for 14 days by default and starts with 2 apps and a 4 GB Compute Pool.

While you are on trial you can raise each dimension up to a ceiling, by default 10 apps and 16 GB, without setting billing up.
Hours you accrue during the trial are recorded, so you can see what you would have paid, and are never invoiced.

The exact figures that apply to your organization are shown on the **Billing** page.

## Setting billing up

Setting billing up means completing checkout, so that we hold a customer record and a live subscription for your organization.

That single fact does three things:

- It lifts the trial ceiling, up to the self-service limit.
- It lets your monthly statements be issued.
- It keeps your apps running when the trial ends.

Buying a subscription ends the trial immediately, and normal metering applies from then on.

## If the trial ends without billing

Your apps are stopped.
Every live instance is suspended and your selection is set to zero, so a stopped organization accrues no charges for capacity it cannot use.

Nothing is deleted.
Setting billing up restores both the instances and the selection you last chose yourself.

## Subscription states

| State | Use instances | Provision new instances | Notes |
| --- | :---: | :---: | --- |
| Trialing | ✅ | ✅ | Trial ceiling applies until billing is set up. |
| Active | ✅ | ✅ | Normal operation. |
| Past due | ✅ | ❌ | A payment failed. Instances keep running. |
| Grace period | Read-only | ❌ | Instances are forced read-only. |
| Suspended | ❌ | ❌ | Instances are not reachable. |
| Cancelled | ❌ | ❌ | Instances are not reachable. |

## How a failed payment escalates

A subscription that stays unpaid moves down the ladder on a timer, not on any single payment event.

```
active → past due → grace period → suspended → deleted
```

Past due becomes grace period once the grace window has passed since your paid period ended, 7 days by default.
Each step after that takes the retention window, 14 days by default.

<Aside type="tip">
Fixing the payment at any point before deletion restores your instances.
Update your payment method through the customer portal link on the **Billing** page.
</Aside>

Members are managed per organization under **Members**.
Owners and admins can invite people and change roles.
Members can see the roster but cannot change it.

## Roles

| Capability | Owner | Admin | Member |
| --- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| View members and instances | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Open an instance they are assigned to | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Invite, remove, and re-role members | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| Grant the owner role | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Administer an owner | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Create, upgrade, and delete instances | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| Grant instance access | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| Manage billing | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| Read the audit log | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| Configure single sign-on | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| Delete the organization | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |

An admin can administer members and other admins, but never an owner.
An organization must always keep at least one owner, so the last owner cannot be demoted or removed.

## Invite a member

<Steps>

1. Go to **Members** and enter the person's email address.

2. Choose the role they should get.

3. Send the invitation.

</Steps>

Invitations expire after 7 days.
The invited person accepts under **Organizations** the next time they sign in, whether or not the email reached them.

<Aside type="note">
An invitation is addressed to an email address, not to an account.
Only the person who signs in with that address can accept it.
</Aside>

## Change a role or remove a member

Use the row menu next to a member.

Anyone can remove themselves, which is how you leave an organization.
Removing a member revokes their instance access with their membership.

## Revoke an invitation

Pending invitations are listed under the member roster.
Revoking one stops it being accepted, even if the recipient still holds the email.

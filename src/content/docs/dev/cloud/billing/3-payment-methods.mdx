Ricochet Cloud charges your organization once a month for the hours used.
Statements are listed on the **Billing** page.

## Card

Card is the default.
Each monthly statement is charged to the card on your subscription.
Update the card and view your payment history through the customer portal link on the **Billing** page.

## Invoice

<Aside type="note">
  Invoicing is a credit of trust from our side.
  We are happy to provide this option for **verified businesses** which prefer invoicing or cannot use credit card payments.
</Aside>

You can request to pay by invoice on 30-day terms.

<Steps>

1. On the **Billing** page, request invoicing.

2. Enter your registered company name and a company website or public registry URL.

3. Submit the request.

</Steps>

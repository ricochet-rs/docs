Membership of an organization does not give access to its instances.
Owners and admins grant access per member, per instance.
With an [external OIDC provider](/dev/cloud/instances/6-authentication/), sign-in is controlled by the provider's application assignments instead.

## Grant or revoke access

<Steps>

1. Open the instance card and select **Access**.

2. Toggle each member on or off.

</Steps>

The person who creates an instance is assigned to it automatically.
Everyone else, including other owners, starts with no access.

At the end of each month, ricochet Cloud writes a statement for your organization with one line per metered dimension.
Statements are listed on the **Billing** page and can be downloaded.

## How a statement is priced

Billable hours are summed for the month and priced at the rates in force, in EUR by default.
The rates and your billing address are snapshotted onto the statement when it is written.

A later rate change never reprices a closed month.

Hours inside your trial window are recorded but never billed.

## Statement states

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Issued | The statement is final and ready to be charged. |
| Waiting on billing | The month is computed, but billing is not set up yet. Set it up and the statement is issued on the next run. |
| Void | The statement no longer applies. |

Payment is tracked separately from the document, because a statement can be a complete record long before it is charged:

| Payment | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Not charged | Issued, not yet charged. |
| Charging | The charge is in flight. |
| Invoiced | Billed on payment terms, and legitimately unpaid until they lapse. |
| Paid | Settled. |
| Payment failed | The charge did not go through. |

## Paying by card

Card is the default.
Statements are charged against the payment method on your subscription.

Update the card, and read your payment history, through the customer portal link on the **Billing** page.

## Paying by invoice

Net terms are credit we extend, so invoicing is requested by you and granted by us.

<Steps>

1. On the **Billing** page, request invoicing.

2. Enter your registered company name and a company website or public registry URL.

3. Submit the request.

</Steps>

Your organization keeps paying by card while the request is reviewed, so nothing is interrupted.
You can withdraw a pending request at any time.

Once granted, statements are billed on terms, 30 days by default.
Only we can grant, decline, or revoke invoicing.

<Aside type="note">
If a request is declined, the reason is shown on the **Billing** page.
</Aside>

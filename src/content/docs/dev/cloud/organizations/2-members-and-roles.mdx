Members are managed per organization under **Members**.
Owners and admins can invite people and change roles.

## Roles

| Capability                                | Owner | Admin | Member |
| ----------------------------------------- | :---: | :---: | :----: |
| View members and instances                |  ✅   |  ✅   |   ✅   |
| Open an instance they are assigned to     |  ✅   |  ✅   |   ✅   |
| Invite, remove, and re-role members       |  ✅   |  ✅   |   ❌   |
| Grant the owner role                      |  ✅   |  ❌   |   ❌   |
| Administer an owner                       |  ✅   |  ❌   |   ❌   |
| Create, upgrade, and delete instances     |  ✅   |  ✅   |   ❌   |
| Grant instance access                     |  ✅   |  ✅   |   ❌   |
| Manage billing                            |  ✅   |  ✅   |   ❌   |
| Read the audit log                        |  ✅   |  ✅   |   ❌   |
| Configure an instance's identity provider |  ✅   |  ✅   |   ❌   |
| Delete the organization                   |  ✅   |  ❌   |   ❌   |

An admin can administer members and other admins, but never an owner.
An organization must always keep at least one owner, so the last owner cannot be demoted or removed.

## Invite a member

<Steps>

1. Go to **Members** and enter the person's email address.

2. Choose the role they should get.

3. Send the invitation.

</Steps>

Invitations expire after 7 days.
The invited person accepts under **Organizations** after signing in with the invited email address.

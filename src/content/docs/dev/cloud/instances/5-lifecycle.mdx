---
title: Instance Lifecycle
---

The status badge on an instance card shows what the instance is doing.

**Provisioning**, **Upgrading**, and **Deleting** resolve on their own.
**Read-only** and **Suspended** come from your organization's [subscription](/dev/cloud/billing/1-subscription/) and clear once the billing issue is resolved.
**Failed** means provisioning did not complete, so contact support if it persists.

## Delete an instance

Owners and admins delete an instance from its card.
Deleting an instance deletes its data, so take what you need off it first.
